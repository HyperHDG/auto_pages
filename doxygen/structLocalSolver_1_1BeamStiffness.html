Detailed Description

Layout of the Timoshenko beam: the frame of the beam and its six stiffnesses.

In the frame of the beam (tangent, two normals) the local problem falls into four independent ones: stretching (u_0), twisting (r_0), and bending in the two planes, which couples the transverse displacement with the rotation about the other normal, (u_2, r_1) and (u_1, r_2). Without a load, the fluxes of each are in balance and vanish for rigid motions, so the condensed operator of a problem is fixed by few numbers: with the differences d(.) between the two nodes, stretching: flux = +- k_a d(u_0), twisting likewise with k_t and r_0, bending: shear force = +- k_s e_s, e_s = d(u) - h (r at node 0 + r at node 1), moment = +- k_b d(r) - h k_s e_s, (upper sign at node 1) with h = +- half the length of the beam. The stiffnesses are positive, so the condensed operator is positive semi-definite (definite with constraints that exclude the rigid motions of the network). The operator starts with the frame, h and the six stiffnesses, which the solver computes from the four small local problems (TimoshenkoBeam::beam_operator): 16 numbers, two cache lines in double, whatever the degree, and all that apply reads. apply rotates the traces into the frame, applies the formulas and rotates back.

The residual also needs the fluxes of the local load, K l with K = C A^-1. K falls into the same four parts, 40 n numbers for n shape functions, which the operator keeps behind the 16 (about a quarter of the size of Condensed in total), so that the residual factorizes nothing. local_solution, for errors, solves the four problems of its beam on the fly.
