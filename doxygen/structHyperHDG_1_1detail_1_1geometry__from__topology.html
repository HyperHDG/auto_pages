Detailed Description

template<typename GlobalLoopT>
struct HyperHDG::detail::geometry_from_topology< GlobalLoopT >

Whether the geometry of a global loop is constructed from (and refers to) a topology.

The global loops are templates GlobalLoopT<TopologyT, GeometryT, NodeDescriptorT, LocalSolverT, LargeVecT, dof_index_t>. Their constructor (topology, geometry, local solver) passes the second argument to the constructor of GeometryT. The file based geometries are constructed from a topology, a reference to whose domain information they keep, whereas the cubic geometries copy the vector of elements. For other global loops, the answer is true (to be safe).
