Detailed Description

template<unsigned int space_dimT, typename param_float_t = double>
struct tests::DiffusionSin< space_dimT, param_float_t >

u = sin(pi/2 x_0) on the unit cube, Dirichlet data everywhere.

TestParametersSinEllipt of reproducibles_python/parameters/diffusion.hxx, the problem of the former reproducibles_python/diffusion_convergence_elliptic.py. The coefficients do not depend on space, so identical hyperedges share one local operator (cf. Diffusion::operator_slot).
