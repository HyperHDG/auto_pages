Detailed Description

The averaging map Pi_eps applied to discrete eigenfunctions of the cross section.

For plate i, p_i(r) is the average of w over the thickness at distance r from the junction,
extended to (0, 2 eps) by reflection at r = 2 eps (extension="reflection"), and r_N is the
average over the junction. extension="zero" extends by zero instead, which does not map into H^1
(only used for comparison). The functions are sampled for common.gap, i.e., the inner product of
H_0 is the Euclidean product of the samples (without the common factor int cos^2(k pi s) ds):
for every plate, the thickness averages at the Gauss points of (2 eps, 1) and, for the collar
(0, 2 eps), at the reflected Gauss points of (2 eps, 4 eps), and the junction average.
