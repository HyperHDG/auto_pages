inline

The operator of the layout BeamStiffness: the frame of the beam and its stiffnesses.

The stiffnesses are the responses to unit differences of the traces between the two nodes (two solves of the four parts); the fluxes of loads take twelve transposed solves. Debug builds check what the formulas of the operator rest on: no flux for rigid motions, and no coupling of shear and curvature.

Evaluate the local solution at tensorial products of abscissas.

The components are those of the local unknowns: n (0 to 2), m (3 to 5), u (6 to 8) and r (9 to 11). The stress resultant and the moment are given in the frame of the beam (along its tangent and its two normals), the displacement and the rotation in space.
