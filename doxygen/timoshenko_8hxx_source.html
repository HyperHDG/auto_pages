9 * \file timoshenko.hxx
10 * \brief Test problems for the Timoshenko beam solver on networks of straight beams.
11 *
12 * From the former reproducibles_python/parameters/timoshenko.hxx (notation of Hauck, Malqvist,
13 *Rupp, "Arbitrary order approximations at constant cost for Timoshenko beam network models" (2024),
14 * eq. (2.2)): on an edge with unit tangent i, the displacement u, rotation r, stress resultant n
15 * and moment m satisfy, with unit coefficients,
16 * -(d_x u + i x r) = n, -d_x r = m, d_x n = f, d_x m + i x n = g,
17 * with continuity of u, r and balance of n, m at the nodes. The rotation variable phi of the solver
18 * is -r, right_hand_side_n is f and right_hand_side_m is -g. The parameter functions receive a
19 *point and a direction and return the component of the vector field along it.
20 **************************************************************************************************/

23/*!*************************************************************************************************
24 * \brief Manufactured solution on the cross of beams along the x and the y axis
25 * (domains/cross.geo; also the single beam along y of domains/line.geo).
26 *
27 * u = cos(pi x) e_z + cos(pi y) e_y and r = -(sin(pi x) e_y + sin(pi y) e_z), zero at the four
28 * ends. Beams along x (i = e_x): n = (pi + 1) sin(pi x) e_z, m = pi cos(pi x) e_y, so
29 * f = pi (pi + 1) cos(pi x) e_z and g = -(pi^2 + pi + 1) sin(pi x) e_y. Beams along y
30 * (i = e_y): n = sin(pi y) (pi e_y + e_x), m = pi cos(pi y) e_z, f = pi cos(pi y) (pi e_y + e_x)
31 * and g = -(pi^2 + 1) sin(pi y) e_z. Both beam lines are smooth through the junction, so the
32 * junction conditions hold.
33 **************************************************************************************************/
