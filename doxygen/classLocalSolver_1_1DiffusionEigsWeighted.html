Detailed Description

template<unsigned int hyEdge_dimT, unsigned int poly_deg, unsigned int quad_deg, template< unsigned int, typename > typename parametersT = DiffusionEigsWeightedParametersDefault, typename lSol_float_t = double>
class LocalSolver::DiffusionEigsWeighted< hyEdge_dimT, poly_deg, quad_deg, parametersT, lSol_float_t >

LDG-H method for weighted diffusion eigenvalue problems with hypernode mass.

The local solver discretizes the eigenvalue problem

\[ - \nabla \cdot ( d \kappa \nabla u ) + d c u = \lambda d \rho u \quad \text{ in } E, \qquad \sum_{E \ni N} d \kappa \nabla u \cdot \nu_E = \lambda \mu_N u \quad \text{ on } N, \]

on every hyperedge \(E\) and every hypernode \(N\) that carries mass, where \(\nu_E\) is the outward unit normal (conormal) of \(E\), and the hypernodes of Dirichlet type have homogeneous Dirichlet values. The plate weight \(d\), the diffusion coefficient \(\kappa\), the mass density \(\rho\), the reaction coefficient \(c\), and the contribution \(\mu\) of the hyperedge to the mass density \(\mu_N\) of its mass hypernodes are constant on every hyperedge of the initial (unrefined) hypergraph. They are read from the hyperedge properties (keyword HYPEREDGE_PROPERTIES of a .geo file) in the order \((d,\kappa,\rho,c,\mu)\); missing entries default to \((1,1,1,0,0)\). Thus, \(\mu_N\) is the sum of \(\mu\) over all hyperedges that contain \(N\).

In contrast to the other eigenvalue local solvers, the bulk unknowns are not eliminated. Only the flux \(q = - \kappa \nabla u\) is eliminated, i.e., for \((w, \hat w)\) in the space of bulk and skeletal unknowns, \(Q(w,\hat w)\) is the local solution of

\[ (\kappa^{-1} Q(w,\hat w), s)_E = (w, \nabla \cdot s)_E - \langle \hat w, s \cdot \nu \rangle_{ \partial E} \]

for all discrete fluxes \(s\). Then, the eigenvalue problem is the linear and generalized eigenvalue problem \(a_h(w, v) = \lambda m_h(w, v)\) with the symmetric bilinear forms

\[ a_h(w, v) = \sum_E d \left[ (\kappa^{-1} Q w, Q v)_E + c (w, v)_E + \tau \langle w - \hat w, v - \hat v \rangle_{\partial E} \right], \qquad m_h(w, v) = \sum_E d (\rho w, v)_E + \sum_N \mu_N \langle \hat w, \hat v \rangle_N. \]

This local solver provides the local matrices of these forms, which are assembled by GlobalLoop::GeneralizedEigenvalue. The bulk unknowns and the flux are tensor-product polynomials of degree poly_deg in each direction, and the skeletal unknowns are tensor-product polynomials of the same degree on every face.

Template Parameters
hyEdge_dimTDimension of a hyperedge, i.e., 1 is for PDEs defined on graphs, 2 is for PDEs defined on surfaces, and 3 is for PDEs defined on volumes.
poly_degThe polynomial degree of test and trial functions.
quad_degThe order of the quadrature rule.
parametersTStruct depending on templates space_dimTP and lSol_float_TP that contains the arrays dirichlet_nodes and (optionally) mass_nodes.

Evaluate the local reconstruction of an eigenfunction at tensorial abscissas.

This function is used for plotting: The skeletal values of an eigenfunction and the eigenvalue determine its bulk values by the local equations \((A_{uu} - \lambda M_{uu}) u = - A_{u\hat u} \hat u\) (if the eigenvalue is not an eigenvalue of the local problem). The flux is reconstructed from the bulk and skeletal values.

lambda_valuesThe values of the skeletal variable's coefficients.
hyper_edgeThe hyperedge.
eigenvalueThe eigenvalue (passed as time by the plot functions).
Return values

The coefficients of \(Q(w, \hat w)\) are \(M_q^{-1} G (w, \hat w)\), where \(M_q\) is the (weighted) mass matrix of the flux, and \(G\) is returned by this function.
