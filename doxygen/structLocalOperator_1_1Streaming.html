Detailed Description

Nothing is stored: every call sets up the local problem of its hyperedge, factorizes it, uses the factors and drops them (the Factored operator, for one use).

The operator is empty, so a prepared loop keeps one for all hyperedges and nothing per hyperedge; its results depend on the hyperedge it is applied to.
