Detailed Description

The condensed operator: S = C A^-1 B + D and K = C A^-1, no factors.

Building costs a factorization, a solve with n_loc right-hand sides, and one with A^T for K; applying costs one product with S (n_loc^2 instead of about 2 n_ldofs n_loc + n_ldofs^2 for Factored), the residual one with K in addition. Smaller than Factored, since n_loc < n_ldofs. No local_solution: reductions that need it build the Factored operator on the fly. Expensive to build: without prepare (an operator per hyperedge and sweep), Factored is the cheaper one.
